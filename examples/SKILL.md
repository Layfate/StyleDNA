## Visual language

A manually authored study of flat organic forms and a restrained earth palette.

- **Lighting:** Flat ambient illumination without directional shadows
- **Shapes:** Rounded architectural masses balanced with simple organic forms
- **Rendering:** Matte, flat color fields with clean silhouettes

## Generation rules

- Use broad, simplified organic forms
- Keep generous negative space

## Negative constraints

- Avoid photographic detail
