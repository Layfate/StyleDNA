# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome different experience levels, give concrete and constructive feedback, and assume good intent while addressing harmful behavior promptly.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized content directed at participants, and publishing private information without consent are unacceptable. Keep project spaces focused on useful collaboration.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or ban participants for violations, considering context and impact. Reports should be sent privately to a repository maintainer through an available private contact channel; do not post private details publicly. Maintainers should acknowledge reports, protect the reporter's privacy, and disclose conflicts of interest.
